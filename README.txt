SUMMER BISTRO — THE LIVING SUNRISE (DEMO)

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js

How to preview:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open this folder in VS Code.
3. Open index.html in a browser, or use the Live Server extension.

Design notes:
- No café photos or supplied café images are included.
- Visuals are CSS-generated abstract illustrations.
- The DEMO label is visible.
- Menu items and prices are sample placeholders and must be replaced with verified details.
- No real booking, ordering, or messaging is performed.
- Google Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts when internet is available; fallback fonts are defined.

Before launch:
Confirm the café's preferred brand spelling, menu, prices, official address, opening hours, social links, and any reservation/order links.
